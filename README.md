## Critical scientific/ownership boundary

The owner's personal map and AI research maps are **not interchangeable**. AI may author separate reference research, but it may not mark a personal node human-understood, owner-approved, or human-verified without explicit, specific owner review. An executed simulation is not experimental validation; a drafted citation is not necessarily a human-verified citation.
