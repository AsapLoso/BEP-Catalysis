### Phase-shift clarification for slide 8

- Unwrapped ideal lag is 360° for the chosen period; the upwind-MOL lag is 358.692644°, giving a **+1.307356° phase lead error** relative to the ideal delayed response.
- The rising zero crossing occurs at normalized time 0.996368456 rather than 1. The inset magnifies this interval; both signals retain the prescribed forcing frequency.
- Curves are steady harmonic responses, not trajectories initialized at a shared zero state. A changing frequency or accumulated time-integrator phase drift is not demonstrated by this exact-time forced linear calculation.
- Both amplitude and phase are generated directly from the stated transfer function. A separate comparison is needed to quantify time-integration errors of BDF, Euler or another method.
