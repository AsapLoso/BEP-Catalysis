## Reliable scientific facts

- **The objective is an open-loop predictor.** The written proposal couples spatial transport to dynamic surface coverages under periodic inlet concentration forcing. It contains broader calibration and optimization ambitions, while observers and MPC are future research. Later conventions and current mission narrow the immediate work; supervisor approval of a revised full deliverable package is not established. [Scope evidence](scope_provenance.md).
- **A reusable framework already exists.** Berger (2008), Figure 5, couples a pseudo-homogeneous axial gas balance to time-dependent surface coverages. Its worked example uses spatial finite differences, MOL, and implicit BDF. This is established methodology; its radial-pellet extension and acetylene-specific parameters need separate justification. [Primary reading record](literature_reuse.md).
- **Direct periodic acetylene research exists.** Urmès et al. (2022) reports a transient fixed-bed microkinetic model under periodic flow conditions. This is a high-priority scientific comparator; full equations, supplements, implementation, and transfer to the intended catalyst remain access/compatibility questions. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/cctc.202101826), [literature comparison](literature_reuse.md).
- **State-space representation is straightforward in principle.** Spatial discretization produces an instantaneous vector `x(t)`; time indexes its evolution. The script allocates 1,480 entries across bulk gas, pore gas, and surface coverages. Allocated entries are not all independent active dynamics because boundary treatment and shell states are defective. [Actual code evidence](simulator_numerics.md).
- **The implementation is a scaffold with demonstrable issues.** Zero default kinetics, a fixed inlet, boundary-state mutation, the radial-centre coefficient, frozen shell coverages, and quadrature bias prevent a validated reacting periodic prediction claim. Static checks were reproduced; no integration or chemical validation was performed during this mission. Readsorption is present, and the alleged double site-capacity factor remains retracted. [Defect/correction register](simulator_numerics.md).
- **A newer implementation exists.** The local sibling `pbr` package offers modular transport, rate laws, callable inlet forcing, and a sparse solver interface. Its optional surface traces do not affect the gas reaction rates; source checks find further sparsity, boundary, and conservation issues. Describe it as a reusable candidate rather than present the older 1,480-entry script as the only implementation or imply the new package validates transient microkinetics. [Implementation addendum](alternate_implementation.md).
- **Large dimension does not itself require new theory or MOR.** The dependency structure is sparse; ordinary Jacobian-aware implicit methods are available. IMEX, reduced models, and multiple local linearizations are exploratory comparisons requiring an accuracy/cost target and a reliable baseline. Willems' LTI trajectory result is not an exact theorem for nonlinear reactor chemistry. [Numerical options](simulator_numerics.md), [theorem/transfer limits](literature_reuse.md).
