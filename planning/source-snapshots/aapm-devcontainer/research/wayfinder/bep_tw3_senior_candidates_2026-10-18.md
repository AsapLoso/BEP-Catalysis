These are three separate 1–5 fields; they are not combined into one ranking, and a high number does not mean the same thing in every field.

This is a public-role proxy, not a citation or quality ranking.
- **Distance from the thesis theme:** higher means farther from catalysis, reactor modelling, and process control at the DIAM-section level. The screen assigns Analysis 2/5, Numerical Mathematics and Applied Probability 3/5, Statistics 4/5, and Discrete Mathematics & Optimization 5/5. These are section proxies, not individual research-profile findings.
- **Career-stage evidence (age not inferred):** 1/5 marks a current professor or emeritus rank as a later-career proxy; 3/5 means this pass did not establish enough individual public career-stage evidence to order the person. No age or approachability estimate is made, and this field cannot establish who would be kind or ask simple questions.
