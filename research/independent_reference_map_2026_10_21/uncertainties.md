# Uncertainties and next scientific questions (v2 enrichment)

This v2 is an evidence map, not a parameterized model. The questions below separate evidence gaps that can change the model from project hypotheses that require tests.

| Priority | Question / uncertainty | What is known now | Why it matters | Next resolving action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| High | Does the 2022 periodic acetylene microkinetic model include spatially resolved axial transport, and what does its SI say? | Publisher abstract and first-page preview report periodic flow oscillations on Pd/α-Al₂O₃, microkinetic estimation, and matching fixed-bed outlet fractions; full paper/SI were not accessible. | Determines how much of the proposal's broad literature-gap claim survives. | Obtain the full article/SI and inspect model equations, spatial discretization, inputs, outputs, and objective. |
| High | What kinetic mechanism applies to the actual PdAg catalyst and operating feed? | Abstract-level sources describe a two-site PdAg steady model (Pachulski 2012) versus single-site monometallic Pd models (Urmès 2019/2022). | Surface states and parameter transfer determine predicted memory and selectivity. | Retrieve the target catalyst data/thesis and compare alternative mechanisms using steady and transient measurements. |
| High | What are the adsorption/desorption/reaction times at target conditions? | Proposal posits microsecond surface response; Zhu 2021 reports a microsecond heater response for Pt/CO temperature pulsing, not acetylene kinetics. | Sets the forcing bandwidth, stiffness and whether dynamic coverage states matter. | Estimate rates from target-catalyst transient experiments; report uncertainty and Jacobian time-scale diagnostics. |
| High | Can available outlet analyzers resolve the forcing cycle? | Proposal mentions GC; time resolution, sample-loop delay, dead volume and flow normalization are unspecified. | Cycle-averaged performance and state observability may be confounded by analyzer filtering. | Characterize step/frequency response and build the observation model before selecting pulse periods. |
| High | What waveform can MFCs and mixing hardware actually produce? | Proposal suggests feed-valve/MFC modulation, but does not specify total-flow constraints, actuator lag, mixing volume, composition simplex, or safety envelope. | A nominal optimized command may not reach the bed or may violate process constraints. | Measure realized bed-inlet waveforms under representative flows and impose hardware/safety constraints in optimization. |
| High | Does periodic operation outperform the best feasible steady point? | No calibrated parameters, simulation results, or experiments were supplied or created. | This is the project's central scientific hypothesis. | Fit a catalyst-specific model; compare on matched constraints with held-out dynamic experiments and uncertainty bands. |
| Medium | Is axial dispersion negligible at the chosen bed scale and forcing bandwidth? | Proposal hypothesizes high Pe; no tracer-derived (D_{ax}) or residence-time curve is supplied. | Dispersion changes front shape, input delay and local nonlinear rates. | Measure tracer/breakthrough response and compare finite-dispersion and plug-flow predictions. |
| Medium | Are pellet and film transport limitations negligible? | Main baseline omits them. | Medium | Is the isothermal reduction valid under composition cycling? | Main proposal fixes temperature; reaction is exothermic, but no transient bed temperature data are given. | Temperature/composition feedback may imitate or overwhelm a surface-memory effect. | Measure bed/pellet temperature and quantify expected kinetic effect before retaining isothermal scope. |

| Medium | Does catalyst aging confound periodic response? | Pd deposit/hydride/carbide evolution is discussed in literature, but target PdAg behavior is not established here. | Cycle-order drift can appear as a periodic gain or kinetic memory. | Use repeated baselines, randomized waveform order, regeneration controls, and time-on-stream states if warranted. |
| Low | Which calibration strategy should be used? | Main.tex proposes spectroscopic bounds then GC fitting; standalone method proposes grey-box/LHS. ## Epistemic limits

- **Published observations:** only what the cited source locators say, under their reported catalyst and conditions.
- **Proposal hypotheses:** intended research claims, not data.
- **Derived under assumptions:** balance, nondimensional and objective expressions whose conventions are stated in `nodes.yaml` and `method.md`.
- **Interpretations:** source-to-project relevance judgments, with transfer boundaries stated.
- **Open/unverified:** access or evidence gaps, especially full 2022 model scope, thesis claims, PdAg transient rates and dynamic performance.

No calibrated kinetic or transport value is available in this v2. No optimum, benefit factor, resonance period, sensor observability result or experimental validation is claimed.
