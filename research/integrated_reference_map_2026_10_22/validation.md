## Limitations of this validation

- No claim-span entailment engine exists. Historical attachments remain unverified unless a direct locator and bounded support claim were examined.
- The finite-volume and global telescoping balances, scalar transport positivity restriction, Fisher-information representation, and period-map stability condition are derivations under their stated assumptions, not symbolic proof artifacts or a tested implementation.
- No target simulator, numerical solver, grid/time refinement, parameter fitting, optimal control run, target data or physical validation is included.
- A connected graph and valid source IDs do not certify literature-search completeness, model uniqueness, parameter identifiability, or a performance improvement.
- Source-specific conflicts, incomplete PDFs/Supporting Information, target-catalyst transfer and project decisions remain documented in the reconciliations, uncertainty and coverage ledgers.
