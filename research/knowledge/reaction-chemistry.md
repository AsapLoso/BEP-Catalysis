# Reaction chemistry and catalyst surface physics

## What is in the record

The shared net reaction network is recorded as:

- R1: C₂H₂ + H₂ → C₂H₄
- R2: C₂H₄ + H₂ → C₂H₆
- R3: C₂H₂ + 2H₂ → C₂H₆

The R1–R3 source-evidence note states that R3 is the stoichiometric sum of R1 and R2. That identity does not establish a third independent kinetic rate or a direct microscopic route from acetylene to ethane. The note records R1–R3 as ideas for later source work, not as approved personal-map children.

## Alternatives that must stay separate

Two different questions appear repeatedly in the source record:

1. **Surface route:** sequential hydrogenation through an adsorbed ethylene-like intermediate versus a distinct vinyl/ethylidene-family alternative.
2. **Encounter model:** Langmuir–Hinshelwood, with surface-bound reactants, versus Eley–Rideal, with a gas–surface encounter.

These are different classification axes. An alternative intermediate family is not the same question as the encounter mechanism. Catalyst identity, support, alloy composition, feed, and conditions may change which source model is relevant.

Other recurring surface questions include adsorption and desorption, ethylene readsorption, site ensembles, saturation, and possible inhibition or poisoning. The personal-map queue keeps these as source-dependent questions. A proposed mechanism or AI reference node does not show that the owner accepted the mechanism or that it describes the target catalyst.
