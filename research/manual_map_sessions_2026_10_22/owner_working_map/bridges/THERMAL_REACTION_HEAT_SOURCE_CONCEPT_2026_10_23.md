## Physical quantity, units and equations

A chemical reaction changes the enthalpy carried by reacting material. For reaction coordinate \(j\), define the **molar reaction enthalpy**

\[
\Delta_r H_j(T)=\sum_i\nu_{ij}\,\overline h_i(T).
\]

Here \(\nu_{ij}\) is the stoichiometric coefficient (negative for reactants, positive for products), and \(\overline h_i\) is the partial molar enthalpy of species \(i\) at the relevant thermodynamic state. For dilute/ideal gas approximations, species molar enthalpies under stated assumptions may suffice. Units are **J mol\(^{-1}\) of reaction extent**. Reference states, temperature, phases and signs must be consistent.

Let \(r_j^{(V)}\) be the **volumetric reaction-progress rate** in \(\mathrm{mol\,m^{-3}\,s^{-1}}\) (based on total bed volume if the energy equation uses bed volume). Then a conventional positive-for-exothermic thermal source is

\[
q'''_{\mathrm{rxn}}=\sum_{j\in\mathcal I}\left(-\Delta_r H_j\right)r_j^{(V)}
\qquad [\mathrm{W\,m^{-3}}].
\]

\(\mathcal I\) is a declared set of independent modeled reaction **kinetic channels**. The factor **\(r_j\)** multiplies the energy released/absorbed *per mole of reaction extent* by the number of moles of reaction proceeding per volume/time. The stoichiometry is already used inside \(\Delta_r H_j\) and separately within the species source \(S_i=\sum_j\nu_{ij}r_j^{(V)}\).

If a reported catalytic rate is per catalyst mass, \(r_j^{(m)}\,[\mathrm{mol\,kg_{cat}^{-1}\,s^{-1}}]\), convert before using a bed-volume PDE:

\[
r_j^{(V)}=\rho_{\mathrm{cat,bed}}r_j^{(m)},\qquad
\rho_{\mathrm{cat,bed}}\;[\mathrm{kg_{cat}\,m_{bed}^{-3}}].
\]

**Avoid a common counting error:** The BEP's net stoichiometric \(R_3=R_1+R_2\) does **not** establish a third independent *kinetic* rate. \(\Delta_r H_3=\Delta_r H_1+\Delta_r H_2\) at the same thermodynamic reference follows from Hess's law, but adding \(\Delta_r H_3 r_3\) on top of rates for R1 and R2 would be unjustified **if** \(r_3\) merely denotes their combined net conversion. A genuine independent direct acetylene-to-ethane kinetic pathway may warrant its own \(r_3\) if it is separately defined and not already included in the other channels.

## How the quantity relates to conservation laws

- **Species balance:** \(S_i=\sum_j\nu_{ij}r_j^{(V)}\) measures net moles of species \(i\) created/consumed per bed volume/time; units \(\mathrm{mol\,m^{-3}\,s^{-1}}\).
- **Heat generation:** \(q'''_{\mathrm{rxn}}=-\sum_j\Delta_rH_jr_j^{(V)}\) measures energy generated by those reactions per bed volume/time; units \(\mathrm{W\,m^{-3}}\).
- **Full energy balance:** thermal storage = net convected/conducted heat + reaction heat + heat exchanged with wall/environment, with consistent sensible/total-enthalpy formulation. The reaction source alone is **not** a PDE, model closure or boundary condition.
- **Isothermal closure:** an actual model may prescribe \(T=T_0\) while chemistry and \(q'''_{\mathrm{rxn}}\) remain nonzero; the energy equation is omitted, or heat extraction becomes the necessary balance quantity.
