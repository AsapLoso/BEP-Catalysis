# Our Reactions

In our reactor, we're looking at three possible hydrogenation reactions:

- **R1:** C₂H₂ + H₂ → C₂H₄ (acetylene → ethylene)
- **R2:** C₂H₄ + H₂ → C₂H₆ (ethylene → ethane)
- **R3:** C₂H₂ + 2 H₂ → C₂H₆ (acetylene → ethane)

**Stoichiometrically, R3 = R1 + R2.** We'll need to look at the reaction mechanisms and rates to see what actually happens.
