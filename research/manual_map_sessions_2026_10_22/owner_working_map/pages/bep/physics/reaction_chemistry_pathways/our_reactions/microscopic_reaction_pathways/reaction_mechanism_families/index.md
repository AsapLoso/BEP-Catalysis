Interpreting the overview: **Path I** converts adsorbed acetylene to adsorbed ethylene; **Path II** releases the desired gaseous ethylene; **Path III** over-hydrogenates adsorbed ethylene toward ethane; **Path IV** involves alternative ethylidene/ethylidyne-like bound species and can produce ethane; **Path V** couples unsaturated species into C₄ and potentially green oil. The *red cross-outs* mark undesirable outcomes the authors want to suppress to improve **selectivity**—**not universal physical impossibility**. The specific catalysts, reaction conditions and primary references matter.
