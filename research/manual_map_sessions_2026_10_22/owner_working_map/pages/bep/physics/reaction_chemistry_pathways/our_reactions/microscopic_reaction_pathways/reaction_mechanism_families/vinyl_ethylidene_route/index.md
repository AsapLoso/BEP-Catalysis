## First distinction to investigate

Both proposed routes initially pass through adsorbed vinyl, C₂H₃*. For a subsequent added H atom, the structural outcome depends on *where it goes*:

- **Route A:** vinyl → adsorbed ethylene, approximately H₂C=CH₂*, which can desorb or undergo further hydrogenation.
- **Route B:** vinyl → surface ethylidene, approximately CH₃–CH* with **a different H arrangement and surface bonding**, which in the Mei-model route can then hydrogenate toward ethane.

Adsorbed ethylene and ethylidene can both have C₂H₄ composition but **they are not the same chemical intermediate**. Formulas and surface-asterisk sketches suppress geometry and electronic structure; do not infer a universal site configuration. Neither possibility independently determines an R3 kinetic rate.
