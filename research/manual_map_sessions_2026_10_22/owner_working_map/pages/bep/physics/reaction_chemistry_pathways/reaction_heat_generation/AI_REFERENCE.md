# Reaction heat generation — chemistry/kinetic lens

A reaction has an enthalpy change **per mole of reaction progress**. The energy released or absorbed *per second per reactor volume* also depends on **how fast that reaction takes place**.

The rate of heat generation is schematically \(q'''_{rxn}=-\sum_j\Delta_rH_j r_j^{(V)}\) when \(r_j^{(V)}\) is measured in moles of **reaction extent** per volume per time. That \(r_j\) is the reaction rate the owner asked about; stoichiometric coefficients are already embedded in \(\Delta_rH_j\), and independently appear in the separate species sources \(S_i=\sum_j\nu_{ij}r_j^{(V)}\). The R3 net stoichiometric identity is not by itself proof of a third independent kinetic channel or reason to double count heat.
