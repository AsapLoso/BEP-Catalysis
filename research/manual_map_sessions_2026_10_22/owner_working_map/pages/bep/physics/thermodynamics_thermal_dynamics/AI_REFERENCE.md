# Thermodynamics & Thermal Dynamics

A reacting system has an **energetic** side as well as a chemical pathway and a reaction rate. Thermodynamics relates states, energy, entropy, equilibrium and reaction enthalpies; thermal dynamics asks how temperature may change when energy is stored, transported, released and removed.

For our BEP, this is the **theoretical Physics branch**. It is not a second copy of the reactor's implementation. The heat of a particular hydrogenation reaction also belongs beside that reaction in *Reaction Chemistry & Pathways*; thermodynamic principles belong here. Heat transport connects to a future *Transport Phenomena* branch.

A future *Reactor Model* branch will record actual modeling choices—such as an imposed temperature or the particular heat-balance equation—and link back to the relevant theory. That model hierarchy has not been selected.
