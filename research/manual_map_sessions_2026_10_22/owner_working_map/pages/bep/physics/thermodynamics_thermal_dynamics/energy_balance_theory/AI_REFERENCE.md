# Energy balance — thermal conservation theory

This is the future theory of energy conservation in a reacting flow: energy can be stored in the bed, brought or removed by fluid convection, conducted/transferred spatially, generated from the reaction chemistry, and exchanged with walls or surroundings.

The general organizing idea is **rate of change of stored energy = net inward energy flux + thermal source terms**, with a declared energy basis and constitutive assumptions. A detailed PDE requires explicit variables, thermal properties, boundary data and consistent reaction-rate/enthalpy units.
