# Isothermal CO-Sensitive Kinetics for a PFR: Source Selection

**Date:** 2026-10-26
**Branch:** `feat/isothermal-co-kinetics-source-selection`
**Decision:** **NO SOURCE READY**

## Decision rule

A source is ready only if its primary publication supports a fixed-temperature gas-phase PFR or packed-bed benchmark with (1) a CO-dependent rate law and complete numerical parameters with units, (2) measured conversion or selectivity at reproducible feed, temperature, pressure, flow and catalyst conditions, and (3) enough matching observations to test a PFR prediction without reconstructing heat transfer or refitting. A PFR calculation using a rate law fitted in another reactor is not an experimental PFR validation.

No source found in this screen meets all three requirements. Bos et al. provide the most useful bounded CO-sensitive rate-law candidate; Hock et al. provide the closest industrial PdAg/CO packed-bed observations. Neither is a ready PFR kinetic benchmark. Schbib et al. are the closest source with a fixed-bed flow experiment, CO data, and a full parameterized law, but the published isothermal PFR calculation does not reproduce all measured species using a verified parameter set.

## Quantitative check available for Schbib

The completed Schbib audit on the parent research branch already contains an executed, fixed-temperature catalyst-mass PFR calculation using the paper's Table 8 law. It is used here as inherited evidence, not described as a new run in this source-selection task. The original printed set predicted C₂H₂ conversions of 0.8409%, 0.0099% and 0.0018% at the three Table 6 conditions, versus 98.31%, 98.99% and 98.62% measured. The separately transcribed author-shared set predicted 99.7854%, 99.9982% and 100.0000% for C₂H₂, but its net C₂H₄ disappearance errors reach 10.763 percentage points. The correction image could not be verified and its kgcat rate basis differs from the printed gcat units. See the [Schbib audit](schbib_parameter_and_benchmark_audit.md) and [source transcription](../evidence/transcriptions/schbib_1996_parameter_tables.md).

Those calculations show why Table 8 is not yet a defensible all-species isothermal PFR benchmark. They do not establish that the original measurements are wrong, identify a corrected parameter set, or justify refitting. No Bos-based PFR calculation was run: without a comparable PFR dataset, such a calculation would be an unvalidated transfer. No thermal model or kinetic refit was performed in this task.

## Recommendation and smallest evidence gap

**Use `NO SOURCE READY` for the requested isothermal CO-sensitive PFR benchmark.** Retain Bos Model 8 as a source-verified candidate law only within its Pd/Al₂O₃ Berty domain. Retain Hock as the closest published industrial PdAg/CO performance dataset. Retain Schbib as a parameterized packed-flow source with documented inconsistencies and thermal-model dependence. None supports a source-validated isothermal PFR prediction today.

The smallest dataset that would close the gap is a fixed-temperature gas-phase packed-bed/PFR series with one catalyst and unchanged feed, pressure, flow and catalyst loading across several CO levels; numerical inlet and outlet flows for C₂H₂, C₂H₄, C₂H₆, H₂ and CO; the full CO-dependent rate law and fitted values/units/uncertainty; and a direct check that the listed temperature is the bed temperature. This would allow an independent isothermal PFR calculation against measured conversion and selectivity without reconstructing heat transfer. PdAg-specific measurements are needed before any CO parameters are transferred to the CO-free PdAg kinetic source.
